Sources

Source 1. Extract from Captain Robert Falcon Scott's diary
17th January 1912

The worst has happened... We arrived at the South Pole today to find the Norwegians have beaten us. Poor Oates, Wilson, and Bowers. After all our effort, we are not first. A black flag marks their achievement. Amundsen's tent sits there, a clear symbol of our defeat. We have done what we came to do, reached the Pole, but it is a bitter disappointment to find we are second. The journey back will be difficult, perhaps impossible. Our supplies are calculated to the minimum. Evans is showing signs of weakness already. The temperature has dropped to -30°F and the weather worsens daily. We shall do our best to return, but I fear for our survival.
